1. Product promise
IDLE 1 is a portrait-first mobile idle strategy RPG designed to be materially faster to build than Empire Lords 
Dragon while still feeling like a complete commercial game. The player alternates between watching automatic 
combat, improving the Lord, growing the Kingdom, expanding a personal Domain and participating in 
asynchronous social competition.
 continuous auto combat with visible kill rewards
 hero gear loot forge and visual equipment progression
 army growth from lone Lord to enormous mixed army
 visible kingdom growth
 personal domain expansion
 research and unit unlocks
 always-on idle events and dungeons
 30-member alliances with chat
 10v10 asynchronous alliance territory wars
Do not dilute - This is not a six-icon idle menu. Progress must be visible on the Lord, the army, the Kingdom 
and the Domain map.

3. Campaign and monster world
The campaign contains 200 stages grouped into 10 visually distinct regions. Each stage has ten waves. Every 
fifth stage is Elite; every tenth is a Boss. The difficulty is server-authored by the canonical enemy-power curve, 
while the client turns the result into a cinematic auto-battle.

4. Hero XP, skills and talents
Hero levels are earned with XP only. There is no second Gold charge for leveling. XP to next level is `round(80 * 
level^1.55 + 40 * level)`. Clearing all 200 campaign stages once supplies about 66.73% of the XP required for level 
100, placing the player around level 85 before repeat/offline/event XP.

Three auto-skill slots unlock at Hero 10, 30 and 60. Talent points arrive every 5 levels. The player chooses among 
Warrior, Guardian, Commander and Fortune branches; respec costs 50 Rubies.
5. Gear, loot and visible equipment
Gear is mandatory in v1.1. The Lord has nine equipment slots: Weapon, Offhand, Helmet, Chest, Gloves, Boots, 
Cloak, Ring and Amulet. Weapon, Helmet, Chest, Boots and Cloak visibly change the battle character. Rarity 
progresses from Common to Ancient as the campaign advances.

6. Forge, salvage and reforge
Forge progression belongs to the equipment slot, not the item. If the player upgrades Weapon Forge to +12 and 
later finds a Legendary sword, the new sword immediately benefits from Weapon Forge +12. This makes 
upgrading feel safe and removes the common idle frustration of wasting materials on replaced gear.

Essence. Reforge changes one selected secondary affix and cannot reduce rarity or primary stats. Auto Equip is 
free. Auto Salvage unlocks at stage 20.

Campaign gates open, recruited units enter the formation and visibly join the battlefield. Real counts are stored 
on the server; rendering uses representative cohorts so late game can look enormous without destroying mobile 
performance.

10. Buildings, research and resources
The v1.0 foundation is retained: 16 buildings, five core soft resources and 48 research nodes with five levels 
each. Battle rewards now supplement - not replace - economic buildings. Personal Domain ownership adds +5% 
production per 10 owned tiles up to +50%.
Exact per-level building and research costs/times remain in CANONICAL_SPEC. The builder must never 
summarize these into guessed curves at runtime.
11. Personal Domain
The personal 10x10 map starts with one owned tile. Every two cleared campaign stages claims one canonical 
adjacent tile until all 100 are owned at stage 200. Roads, farms, towers, villages, forts and city silhouettes 
accumulate so the map becomes visibly denser and more imperial.
12. Offline progression
The server calculates elapsed time using server timestamps. The offline chest holds up to 12 hours. It includes 
production, repeat-stage XP/Gold/soft resources, limited gear rolls and completed 
construction/research/recruitment timers. The device clock is never trusted and each claim is idempotent.

13. Dungeons, events and retention
Four 10-minute idle dungeons unlock progressively with 10 tiers. Each gives two free entries per day; up to 
three extra entries per dungeon cost 25 Rubies each. Weekly events remain always active through a rotating 
event archetype and offer Energy refills, instant finish and Event Pass purchases.
Dungeon Primary reward
Royal Treasury Gold plus soft resources
Forge Depths Forge Dust + Reforge Stones
Ancient Ruins Gear roll + Mythic Essence at high tier
Monster Hunt Hero XP + Event Tokens
Daily tasks provide 10 Rubies total, weekly tasks 50 Rubies, and the 7-day login cycle 35 Rubies plus a boosted 
gear roll. Forty achievements and a Codex add long-term one-time goals.
14. Alliance, chat and Alliance Boss
Alliances unlock at Castle 8, cost 5,000 Gold to create and hold up to 30 members. Chat channels: Global, 
Alliance, War and System Feed. Direct messages remain out of scope. Report, block, mute and moderation are 
mandatory.
Titan Hunt is a 48-hour asynchronous Alliance Boss. Each member receives three free attacks per day; up to 
three extra attacks cost 50 Rubies each. It rewards War Coins, Forge materials and cosmetic progression but 
does not directly change territory.
15. Asynchronous 10v10 territory war
Alliance War remains a 10v10 asynchronous system on a 19x19 competitive map. One adjacent territory is 
attacked, eight hours of preparation run, and ten deterministic lanes resolve. The complete 
Hero/Gear/Forge/Talent/Army/Research snapshot freezes 30 minutes before resolution.
Competitive integrity - Anything purchased or upgraded after roster lock does not change that war. This 
keeps monetization active without allowing a last-second payment to rewrite an already locked battle.

17. Monetization
Monetization is pay-to-accelerate and cosmetic, not paid random gear. Products: Ruby packs, Starter Bundle, 
weekly Event Pass, 28-day Season Pass, resource crates, dungeon entries, event Energy refills, Alliance Boss 
extra attacks and cosmetics. Production UI must display store-localized prices returned by Apple/Google.
No paid loot boxes/gacha. No item can be bought from a random gear chest. Persistent acceleration can improve 
a future roster, but a locked Alliance War snapshot cannot be modified.
18. Account, privacy and payments
iOS and Android use the same account and backend state. Required: email/password, Google, Sign in with Apple, 
verification, recovery, logout-all, export request, in-app account deletion, Privacy/Terms, consent configuration, 
secure token storage, StoreKit/Play Billing verification, restore purchases and refund/revocation reconciliation.
19. Premium medieval-fantasy art direction
The game must look like a premium medieval-fantasy product, not a SaaS interface. Use parchment, stone, 
wood, forged iron, bronze, antique gold, leather, velvet and heraldry. The hero gear, army size, Kingdom and 
Domain must all communicate progression at a glance.
Forbidden - Cyberpunk, sci-fi, neon technology, generic admin-dashboard cards, chibi style, slot-machine 
loot presentation and placeholders presented as final art.
20. Architecture and security
React + TypeScript + Capacitor client; Python/FastAPI/Pydantic backend; MongoDB production persistence. 
Server authority covers combat outcomes, rewards, loot rolls, timers, offline claims, territory, purchases and 
war snapshots. Critical mutations use transactions or crash-safe state machines and idempotency is reserved 
before side effects.
